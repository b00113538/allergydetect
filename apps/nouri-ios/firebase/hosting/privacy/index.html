Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: Nouri stores what you log so it can show you patterns. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, plus date of birth if you choose to add it.
  • What you log: meal photos and ingredients, symptoms and their severity, skin reactions and photos, the products and fabrics you list, notes, and the known allergies you enter.
  • Allergy test results: if you upload a blood test report, the report file and the results you confirm.
  • Notification settings: a push notification token for your device, your reminder time and time zone.

This includes health information. We only use it to provide Nouri's features to you.

How it's used

  • To find patterns between what you eat or touch and how you feel, and to show them to you.
  • To identify likely ingredients in meal photos and read allergy test reports. Photos and reports are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude API for this. Anthropic processes them to return the result and does not use them to train its models.
  • To send you the reminders and summaries you've switched on.
  • To build your Dine Code when you choose to create one.

Your Dine Code

A Dine Code is a link you choose to share. Anyone holding the code can see your first name and the list of foods to avoid, and nothing else. You can revoke a code in the app at any time, and it stops working immediately.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on your device and in Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging), encrypted in transit and at rest. Only your signed-in account can read it.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers above, and only as needed to run the app: Google Firebase for storage, sign-in and notifications, and Anthropic for photo and report analysis. We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Insights → Profile → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, logs, photos, reports and Dine Codes from our servers and from your device.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Questions or data requests: email [contact email].

Not medical advice

Nouri shows patterns in what you log. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose allergies. Talk to a clinician before changing your diet or treatment.

Children

Nouri is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
