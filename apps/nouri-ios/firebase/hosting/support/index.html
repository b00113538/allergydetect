How does Nouri find triggers?

For each ingredient, product or fabric, Nouri counts how often it came before a reaction. An item is flagged as a likely trigger once it has appeared at least 3 times and was followed by a reaction at least 70% of the time, more often than your usual rate. The more you log, including days when you feel fine, the more accurate it gets.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that notifications for Nouri are on in iOS Settings and under Profile → Notifications. The evening reminder only arrives on days when you haven't logged anything.

What is a Dine Code?

A QR code you can show at a restaurant. Staff scan it to see what you need to avoid, without installing anything. You can create a new code, or revoke the old one, at any time.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Insights → Profile → Delete account. Everything is permanently erased. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nouri shows patterns in what you log and doesn't diagnose allergies. Always talk to a clinician before changing your diet or treatment.
